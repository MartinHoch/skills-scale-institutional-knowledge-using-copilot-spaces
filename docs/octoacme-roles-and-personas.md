# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and help the team continuously improve their delivery practices. They act as servant-leaders, coaching the team on self-organization and iterative delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on agile practices and iterative delivery
- Maintain sprint health and team velocity metrics
- Escalate cross-team dependencies and risks

### Goals
- Enable consistent sprint execution
- Reduce cycle time and improve team predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- 1:1 coaching sessions with team members
- Escalation notes and blocker tracking

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to identify and resolve blockers
- Supports Developers in maintaining team velocity and sustainable pace
- Collaborates with Product Managers to optimize sprint priorities
- Escalates risks and dependencies to Project Manager and stakeholders

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, design technical solutions, and ensure system quality. They mentor developers and make critical technical decisions that impact long-term maintainability and scalability.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approaches for major features
- Conduct technical reviews and code reviews for complex changes
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Document technical decisions and maintain architecture records

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and reliable systems
- Reduce technical debt and improve system observability
- Build team technical capability through mentoring

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture reviews
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Discussions during sprint planning on technical feasibility

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Developers to guide implementation of complex features
- Advises Project Manager on technical risks and feasibility
- Collaborates with Product Manager on architecture implications of feature requests
- Works with Quality Assurance Lead on testability of architectural decisions

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own test strategy, quality metrics, and acceptance testing. They collaborate with product and development to ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test strategy and test plans
- Coordinate acceptance testing and UAT activities
- Track quality metrics and defect trends
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria
- Identify gaps in testing approach and propose improvements

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards before release
- Reduce defects in production
- Build confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality reports
- Defect logs and quality dashboards
- Participation in sprint planning and release reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers on testability and test automation
- Collaborates with Product Manager to clarify acceptance criteria
- Supports Project Manager with quality metrics and release readiness assessment
- Partners with Technical Lead on test strategy for complex architectures

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge stakeholders and the development team by refining requirements, validating user stories, and ensuring solutions meet business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Refine user stories and acceptance criteria
- Validate that proposed solutions meet business needs
- Identify and communicate scope trade-offs
- Facilitate discussions between business and technical teams

### Goals
- Ensure solutions deliver business value
- Reduce scope creep and requirements rework
- Bridge communication gaps between stakeholders and developers

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user story refinement
- Stakeholder interviews and validation sessions
- Collaboration with Product Manager on prioritization

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Product Manager to define and prioritize requirements
- Collaborates with Developers to clarify technical feasibility
- Supports Project Manager in managing scope and stakeholder expectations
- Partners with Stakeholder Representative to validate business requirements

---

## Sponsor / Executive

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic direction, secure resources, and approve major decisions. They champion the project and ensure alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic direction and business justification for the project
- Secure and allocate resources (budget, people, time)
- Approve major scope changes and trade-offs
- Remove high-level blockers and organizational impediments
- Communicate project status to executive leadership

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value aligned with strategy
- Maintain executive support and resource commitment
- Enable team success by removing organizational barriers

### Typical Communication
- Executive steering committee meetings
- High-level status reports and decision requests
- Escalation for organizational roadblocks

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports Project Manager with resource allocation and escalations
- Receives prioritization and impact guidance from Product Manager
- Approves major decisions and scope changes
- Communicates project vision and business value to organization

---

## Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives provide domain expertise, validate solutions, and communicate with business units. They serve as the voice of the customer or business area.

### Responsibilities
- Provide domain expertise and business context
- Validate that solutions meet stakeholder needs
- Communicate project progress to their business unit
- Identify impacts and dependencies within their area
- Provide feedback on prototypes and demos

### Goals
- Ensure solutions meet real business needs
- Build stakeholder confidence and adoption
- Reduce rework due to misaligned expectations

### Typical Communication
- Participation in demos and validation reviews
- Feedback sessions and requirement sign-offs
- Communication to their business unit on project progress

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Product Manager on requirements and priorities
- Provides input to Business Analyst on requirements validation
- Receives demos and updates from Project Manager
- Works with Quality Assurance Lead to validate acceptance criteria
